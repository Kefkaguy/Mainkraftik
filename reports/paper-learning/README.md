# Two-account paper learning experiment — November 18, 2026

The operator explicitly authorized replacing the deployed paper sizing and entry restrictions and adding the second supplied PAPER account. No live-money endpoint is supported. This is an unvalidated paper experiment, not a newly validated profitable strategy. Phase 7/8 negative findings remain authoritative and their artifacts are preserved.

## Experiment declared before activation

- PA3NSJAY7: the existing deployed Mainkraft numerical model, held fixed. Its original UBS holding is included in subsequent model allocation changes; the previous isolated $800 swing policy no longer manages that account while experiment mode is enabled.
- PA3D4GIWG: the same numerical prior plus online residual ridge regression (fixed alpha 100). Only previously issued forecasts with genuinely matured outcomes can train it. A seeded 10% log-weight perturbation explores relative allocation; it does not randomly reverse predicted direction. No neural network has been fine-tuned or promoted in this release.
- Price forecasts use a declared **uncalibrated one-trading-hour proxy**: the frozen daily gross-return estimate divided by 6.5. Uncertainty is scaled by sqrt(6.5). This transformation is an experimental starting point, not evidence that daily skill transfers to an hourly horizon.
- Inputs: 11 existing causal daily stock/SPY/XLK features, current stock and SPY returns, observed IEX spread, intercept. Daily features exclude the current session. The existing sourced premarket news discovery continues as a separate task; news stance does not veto execution. Six established symbols, latest research candidates, and existing holdings form the universe.
- Rebalance every five minutes while the broker reports the regular market open. Long and eligible whole-share short positions are supported; sign reversals close before opening the other direction. There is no fixed holding deadline, 2% stop, 3% target, one-trade rule, confidence/news cutoff, application dollar cap, position-count cap, or daily-loss cutoff in this mode. Positions can persist overnight. Model allocation changes determine reductions and exits.
- Relative allocation uses expected-return magnitude after an observed-spread plus 10bp cost allowance, divided by uncertainty. It deliberately permits exploration even when no positive edge is established. Gross targets normalize to currently available broker Reg-T buying power plus current gross exposure; margin and substantial paper drawdowns are possible. Broker/account restrictions still apply. No unlimited buying power is fabricated.
- Forecasts, features, source identity, issuance quotes, matured outcomes, model updates, order versions, errors, and account snapshots are recorded separately and append-only. A database cycle marker and stable broker client IDs prevent duplicate submissions after restart. Unknown acknowledgements are reconciled, never blindly resubmitted. Partial-fill quantities cannot regress. Confirmed HTTP rejections retain the broker reason and do not masquerade as fills.
- At issuance, record a fresh midpoint; resolve at one trading hour, carrying across exchange closures, only with a quote timestamp in the following five-minute window. Explicitly record unavailable outcomes. **Forecast labels are hypothetical midpoint returns**, not executed or cost-adjusted trade profits. They cannot establish a profitable edge. Actual Alpaca Paper positions, quantities, average fills, unrealized P/L, and equity changes remain separate.
- Model update coefficients and sample identities are immutable. No unresolved label or future price can train an earlier forecast. The fixed model supplies a control, but different leverage permissions, initial holdings, and equity mean the comparison is observational, not a randomized proof of learning efficacy.

## What remains enforced

Pinned distinct paper account identities; a literal paper API host; authenticated dashboard/control; valid finite quantities and forecasts; fresh non-crossed positive quotes; exchange calendar and broker clock; broker asset permissions and buying power; order deduplication, reconciliation, and operator pause. These protect correctness rather than suppressing a trade merely to meet an arbitrary app cap.

Pause prevents new submissions. Existing orders and positions remain open and visible; pausing is not liquidation. A request already in flight can acknowledge after pause. Unknown broker acknowledgements require reconciliation or operator investigation before that account reallocates.

## How to judge the result

Compare normalized equity changes and gross exposure, actual fill-to-fill returns after fees/financing, turnover, drawdown, rejected/partial orders, quote coverage, forecast error, directional skill, and missing outcome counts. The dashboard exposes observed balances, positions, orders, forecasts and error/learning evidence. Its equity change is a broker mark from activation, not a realized-trade ledger; deposits or account resets invalidate that comparison. Midpoint forecast accuracy is not trading profitability. Historical neural convergence warnings remain visible in regression evidence and were not cured by adding an online residual model.

There is currently **no independently validated profitable trading advantage**. More capital or more paper trades do not establish one. Future neural retraining, calibration, longer-horizon experiments, execution-cost learning, and candidate promotion need their own causal training/evaluation evidence; this release does not claim they have been completed.

The residual learner currently refits its accumulated outcome history. Long-running collection requires monitoring database growth, memory, runtime, and eventually moving the same fit to incremental sufficient statistics or a preregistered rolling training window. Quote outages can leave outcomes explicitly unscored. Neither background adaptation nor the initial two-account comparison supplies an independent profitability certification.
